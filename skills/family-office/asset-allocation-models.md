# Strategic Asset Allocation Models

_Endowment-style ("Yale model") policy portfolios, tiered by objective. These are
starting archetypes — tune every weight to the principal's liquidity needs, jurisdiction,
and risk tolerance in the IPS. Illiquid sleeves must never exceed what the liquidity
ladder can support._

> ⚠️ Educational archetypes, not financial advice or a recommendation to buy any asset.

## Philosophy
- **The allocation is the strategy.** It drives ~90% of return variance — set it deliberately.
- **Core–satellite:** cheap, tax-efficient index core + high-conviction satellites (private
  markets, hedge funds, direct deals) where illiquidity and skill are actually paid for.
- **Harvest the illiquidity premium** only with capital you do not need for near-term spend.
- **Rebalancing > forecasting:** systematic rebalancing is an unemotional source of return.

## Archetypes

| Sleeve | Aggressive growth | Balanced | Preserve + income | Multi-generational |
|--------|------------------:|---------:|------------------:|-------------------:|
| Global public equity | 45% | 35% | 25% | 38% |
| Private equity / VC | 20% | 12% | 5% | 15% |
| Private credit / real assets | 12% | 15% | 15% | 15% |
| Hedge funds / diversifiers | 10% | 15% | 15% | 12% |
| Fixed income / cash | 8% | 18% | 35% | 15% |
| Opportunistic / direct deals | 5% | 5% | 5% | 5% |
| **Illiquid (private) total** | **37%** | **32%** | **25%** | **35%** |

## Rebalancing bands
- Public sleeves: ±5% around target → rebalance when breached.
- Private sleeves: managed via commitment pacing (you cannot rebalance illiquids on demand).
- Review quarterly; act on breaches, not on forecasts.

## Tax-aware asset location (which sleeve, which wrapper)
| Sleeve | Preferred location | Why |
|--------|--------------------|-----|
| Bonds / hedge funds (high ordinary income) | Tax-advantaged / deferred wrappers | Shelters ordinary-income drag |
| Equities (long-term gains) | Taxable, held long | Favorable long-term treatment + harvesting |
| Private equity / real assets | Entity/trust per counsel | Estate & step-up planning |

## Cost discipline
- Every basis point of fee compounds against you for decades. Index the core; pay for
  skill only where it is demonstrable and net-of-fee positive.
- Audit total fee load (fund fees + platform + advisory) annually.

## How to use
1. Pick the archetype closest to the IPS objective.
2. Adjust for the liquidity ladder (cap illiquids), jurisdiction (tax), and constraints.
3. Record the final targets and bands in Section 4 of the IPS and in `portfolio.md`.
