# Investment Policy Statement (IPS) — Template

_The constitution of the family office. Once ratified, decisions are made by this policy,
not by emotion or market noise. Review annually or on a material change in circumstances._

> ⚠️ Educational template, not financial/legal advice. Ratify with a licensed fiduciary.

```markdown

_Version: <n> · Effective: <YYYY-MM-DD> · Next review: <YYYY-MM-DD>_

## 2. Objectives

- Full review annually; interim review on material change. Amendments logged with rationale.
```
